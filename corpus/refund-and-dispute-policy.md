# Refunds and Disputes

## Filing a card dispute
Customers have 60 days from the statement date to file a card dispute. Disputes can be filed in the mobile app, online banking, or by phone.

## Timelines
Provisional credit is issued within 10 business days of a valid dispute. Merchant investigations are completed within 45 days. Approved refunds appear on the statement within 5 business days.

## Not eligible
Transactions already refunded, and authorized recurring payments cancelled after the billing date, are not eligible for dispute.
