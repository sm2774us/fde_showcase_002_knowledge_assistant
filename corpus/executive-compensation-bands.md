# Executive Compensation Bands

## Bands
Band E1 base salary range is $210,000 to $280,000. Band E2 base salary range is $280,000 to $390,000. Band E3 base salary range is $390,000 to $550,000. Executive bonus targets range from 30 to 80 percent of base salary.
